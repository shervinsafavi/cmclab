I am a Master student in Psychology (Cognitive-Affective Neuroscience) with a background in Cognitive Science (B.Sc.). At the CMC Lab I use dimensionality reduction techniques to study prefrontal population dynamics during internally generated perceptual switches (binocular rivalry, multistability) . My broader interests lie in bridging fundamental research with clinical applications, focusing on computational psychiatry, the brain as a dynamical system, and altered states of consciousness.
